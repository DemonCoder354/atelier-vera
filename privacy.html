Privacy Policy

This is a sample policy for a demo website and is not legal advice.

Who we are

Atelier Vera is a fictional fashion studio. You can contact us at hello@ateliervera.com.

What we collect

This website has no forms, no accounts and no analytics. We only receive your name, email address and message if you choose to write to us by email.

How we use it

We use your email only to reply to your message. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security purposes. Fonts are loaded from Bunny Fonts and photos from Unsplash, so these services receive your IP address when the page loads. None of them set cookies through this site.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies, so no cookie banner is needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing hello@ateliervera.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
